P2S (Problem-to-Service) — Reference Citizen Problem Scenarios (Berlin Edition)
================================================================================
Use these realistic everyday problem descriptions to test the system, demo to
professors, and validate your thesis benchmarking pipeline.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOMAIN 1: FACILITY & BUILDING EMERGENCIES (Hausverwaltung & Hausmeister)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  "My Hausmeister is not responding and I need to open the electrical room because the main fuse tripped."
1.2  "Water is leaking through my bathroom ceiling from the apartment upstairs and the neighbor is not home."
1.3  "The main building entrance door lock is jammed and 6 tenants are locked out on the street at midnight."
1.4  "The passenger elevator in our building stopped between the 3rd and 4th floors with someone inside."
1.5  "The central heating in our whole apartment complex stopped working in the middle of January."
1.6  "The basement storage area is flooded with 10cm of dirty water after heavy rain in Berlin."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOMAIN 2: TENANCY & HOUSING LAW (Mietrecht & Berliner Mieterverein)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  "I moved out 8 months ago, did a flawless handover protocol (Übergabeprotokoll), but my landlord still refuses to return my €1,800 Kaution deposit."
2.2  "There is black mold spreading across my bedroom wall and the landlord claims it's my fault for not airing out the room properly (Stoßlüften)."
2.3  "My landlord sent a letter increasing my net cold rent (Kaltmiete) by 25% within 1 year. Isn't this violating the Berlin Mietpreisbremse?"
2.4  "Received an immediate termination letter (Fristlose Kündigung) claiming Eigenbedarf (personal use) for the landlord's distant cousin."
2.5  "The landlord entered my rented apartment with their spare key while I was at university without my permission."
2.6  "Our building has had no warm water for 3 weeks and the landlord ignores my emails. How do I legally declare Mietminderung?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOMAIN 3: HEALTH & MEDICAL CARE (116 117, Notruf & Bereitschaftsdienst)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  "It's Sunday afternoon, all general doctors (Hausärzte) are closed, and I have a 39.5°C fever and severe ear pain. Who can I call?"
3.2  "I need urgent prescription medication for my asthma at 2 AM on a holiday in Berlin. Where is the nearest open pharmacy?"
3.3  "A close friend is going through an acute psychological breakdown, panic attacks, and severe distress in Neukölln."
3.4  "Sprained my ankle badly playing sports in Tiergarten. It's swollen and bruised, but I don't know if I need an ambulance or just an X-ray clinic."

DOMAIN 4: BUREAUCRACY & IMMIGRATION (Bürgeramt 115, LEA Berlin & Visa)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  "My student residence permit (Aufenthaltstitel) expires in 10 days and there are zero open appointments on the LEA Berlin booking portal."
4.2  "Just moved into a new WG room in Berlin, but the landlord refuses to sign the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung for my Bürgeramt Anmeldung."
4.3  "I have been waiting 4 months for my tax ID (Steuer-Identifikationsnummer) from the Finanzamt and my employer is taxing me at Tax Class 6."
4.4  "Graduated from my master's degree and need to transition from student visa § 16b to the 18-month job seeker visa § 20."
4.5  "International student whose blocked account (Sperrkonto) monthly release got frozen due to an administrative document mismatch."

DOMAIN 5: UTILITIES & ENERGY CRISES (Stromnetz Berlin, Wasserbetriebe & Gas)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  "Sudden power blackout across our entire street in Friedrichshain. Streetlights and neighboring buildings are also dark."
5.2  "I smell a strong sulfur/gas odor coming from the basement pipe near the bike cellar." (EMERGENCY)
5.3  "Brown muddy water is coming out of all the taps in our kitchen and bathroom since this morning."
5.4  "Energy supplier sent an annual reconciliation bill (Nachzahlung) demanding €2,400 for a 1-room apartment due to an estimated meter reading error."
5.5  "Gas heating company threatens to cut off the apartment gas supply next Monday over a disputed invoice."
