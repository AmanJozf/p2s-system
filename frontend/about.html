ℹ️ About P2S

Problem-to-Service is an MSc Computer Science thesis project exploring how conversational AI can bridge the gap between everyday citizen problems and the services designed to help them.

What is P2S?

P2S (Problem-to-Service) is an AI-powered triage and service routing system. The core challenge it addresses is what we term the "Terminology Gap" — ordinary people rarely know the bureaucratic or technical language needed to navigate help directories, government portals, or support channels.

Instead of forcing users to know that their problem is a "Tenancy Deposit Protection Scheme Dispute", P2S lets them say "my landlord won't give my money back" — and does all the classification and routing work automatically.

Research Question

The thesis investigates: "To what extent can a structured Large Language Model (LLM) pipeline reduce the cognitive burden on citizens when navigating administrative and technical support services — and how do Cloud vs. Local LLM architectures compare in accuracy, latency, and privacy?"

Methodology

  • Phase 1: Requirement gathering and domain taxonomy design (Housing, IT, Appliances).
  • Phase 2: Structured Output LLM pipeline with constrained JSON generation via Pydantic schemas.
  • Phase 3: Evaluation — comparing Cloud (Gemini) vs Local (Ollama) on accuracy, latency, and privacy.
  • Phase 4: User study — measuring reduction in time-to-resolution vs traditional directory search.
