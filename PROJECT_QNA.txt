================================================================================
P2S (Problem-to-Service) — Quick Q&A Cheat Sheet (Short, Dope & Simple)
================================================================================
Use this when your professor, boss, or examiner asks you tough questions.
Keep your answers short, confident, and straight to the point.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Why choose P2S when we already have ChatGPT and Claude?
A1:
- ChatGPT & Claude give long essays with fake phone numbers and hallucinated laws.
- P2S gives a 4-step checklist and locks recommendations to a VERIFIED database
  of real local organizations. Zero fake contacts.
- Plus, P2S has safety emergency guardrails that trigger immediately for life
  hazards (like gas leaks) instead of writing paragraphs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q2: How does P2S find the right service? Is it just keyword matching?
A2:
- No, it uses Semantic Search (embeddings), not simple keyword matching.
- Keyword matching fails if a user types: "my kitchen is flooded" because the word
  "plumber" or "handwerker" isn't in their sentence.
- Semantic search understands the MEANING and context of words, calculates a vector
  similarity score, and picks the best service with an exact confidence percentage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q3: What problem are you actually solving?
A3:
- The "Terminology Gap".
- People describe pain in everyday emotional words ("my landlord stole my money").
- Bureaucracy hides behind legal jargon ("§ 551 BGB Tenancy Deposit Adjudication").
- P2S translates raw human distress into structured action steps and routes them
  to the exact verified department in under 3 seconds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q4: What happens if the user's problem is super vague (e.g. "my flat is bad")?
A4:
- The system doesn't guess.
- It detects ambiguity, pauses, and asks ONE clear follow-up question with
  clickable options before prescribing any action plan.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q5: What makes this a Master's Thesis in Computer Science, not just a website?
A5:
- It's an empirical study comparing Cloud LLMs (Gemini) vs Local Offline LLMs (Ollama).
- We measure:
  1. Semantic routing accuracy (%)
  2. Latency / Response speed (seconds)
  3. Cost per query
  4. Privacy & data sovereignty (keeping citizen data local on the machine).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q6: What if the internet goes down or the citizen has privacy concerns?
A6:
- P2S supports an Offline Mode using a local model (Ollama).
- Sensitive personal problems never leave the user's computer or local network.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q7: If your app suddenly disappeared, who would actually care?
A7:
- Everyday citizens and students who feel helpless, overwhelmed, and don't know
  who to call in an unfamiliar city.
- Advice bureaus (like Bürgeramt or Mieterverein) whose phonelines get clogged
  with confused people who don't have the right paperwork ready.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q8: What could you remove from the code without losing the core value?
A8:
- The entire multi-page website, charts, and dashboard.
- The core value is the Triage Engine. That engine can run on WhatsApp, SMS, or
  a terminal and still solve the exact same problem.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q9: Why SQLite and not MySQL or PostgreSQL?
A9:
- Zero setup, single-file, lightning fast, and works 100% offline.
- Perfect for local privacy-first edge deployments without running background
  database servers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q10: How does the Voice Dictation work? Is it an external API like AssemblyAI?
A10:
- It uses the Browser's built-in Web Speech API (window.SpeechRecognition).
- This enables instant, zero-latency speech-to-text without sending raw audio to
  third-party cloud servers or requiring external API keys.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q11: Why focus on a specific city like Berlin instead of the whole world/country?
A11:
- The code is universal, but help must be local—emergency numbers, water utilities,
  and housing bureaus are local, so global answers are useless when your pipe bursts.
- Grounding the system in Berlin eliminates hallucinations and proves the
  architecture with 100% verified, working services.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q12: How did you evaluate and test the system? What are your actual results?
A12:
- We built an automated benchmark script (evaluate.py) that tests 21 diverse,
  ground-truth citizen scenarios across all 8 domains and safety edge cases.
- Results (saved in EVALUATION_REPORT.md & evaluation_results.csv):
  1. Domain Accuracy: 95.2% (20/21 test cases correctly matched)
  2. Critical Emergency Recall: 100% (all gas/fire/medical hazards caught instantly)
  3. Average Latency: 1.12 seconds per query.
